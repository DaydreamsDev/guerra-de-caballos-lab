### 2.1 Prediction and calibration (predicción y calibración)

The engineering check is grounded in metacognition research:

| Source | Why it matters here |
|---|---|
| Dunlosky, J., & Metcalfe, J. (2009). *Metacognition.* | Calibration: the gap between confidence and accuracy is a learning signal. |
| Nelson, T. O., & Narens, L. (1990). *Metamemory: A Theoretical Framework.* | Monitoring and control: predict, observe, adjust. |
| Bjork, R. A. (1994). *Memory and Metamemory Considerations in the Training of Human Beings.* | Desirable difficulties: prediction and retrieval strengthen the model. |

Prediction is the **retained form of retrieval**: it produces an answer from memory before the
artifact reveals the truth, without a quiz, a timer or a score.

## 8. Sources deliberately not used for assessment

These are cited for honesty: they are influential, but they belong to a schooling model this project
rejects.

| Source | Why it is not used |
|---|---|
| Bloom, B. S. (1968). *Learning for Mastery.* | Mastery is useful as an idea, but the apparatus becomes grades and tracking. We keep criterion-based progression, not mastery grading. |
| Biggs, J., & Collis, K. (1982). *SOLO Taxonomy.* | A developmental lens, not a score. We replaced it with four self-diagnosis questions. |
| Roediger, H. L., & Karpicke, J. D. (2006). *Test-Enhanced Learning.* | Retrieval is real, but it does not require a quiz. Prediction against a trace and self-explanation keep the mechanism without the exam. |
| Standardized tests and exam banks | They measure recall under artificial constraints and invite ranking. |

## 9. How to cite in the lessons

Every lesson ends with a **“Go deeper”** section with at most three references: one primary, one
practical, one Spanish. The rule is not to impress; it is to give the learner the next step.

```markdown
## Go deeper
- **Primary:** Author (Year). *Title*. — why it matters.
- **Practical:** Author (Year). *Title*. — what to read first.
- **En español:** Author (Year). *Título*. — the same idea in Spanish.
```
